MileStory

  1. Every puzzle starts at an anchor: something that happened on this date, somewhere in the world. Its location is always revealed.
  2. Round 1 is the opening pair. Pick the event that happened closer to the anchor, as the crow flies.
  3. Each round after that brings one incoming event. Tap the gap where it fits on your route.
  4. After every move the true location appears on the map, and your route fixes itself, so one miss never ruins the rest.

Each event has one optional hint. Hints used show up on your result.
