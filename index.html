How to play

  1. Every puzzle starts at an anchor: something that happened on this date, somewhere in the world. The anchor is always revealed.
  2. Round 1 gives you two events with clues but no locations. Pick the one that happened closer to the anchor, as the crow flies.
  3. Each round after that adds one new event. Slot it into your list between the right neighbors.
  4. After every move the true location appears on the map, and the list fixes itself, so one miss never ruins the rest.

Each event has one optional hint. Hints used show up on your result.
